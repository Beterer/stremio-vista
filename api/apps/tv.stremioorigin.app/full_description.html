Stremio Origin: a native app for Stremio addons on LG webOS TVs (ARM).

Bring your own Stremio account and addons: the app ships empty and plays only the links your addons return. Artwork and trailers, the best stream for your TV in one press, styled subtitles and subtitle sync, torrents, 11 languages.

Tested on webOS 4.4. Unofficial: not affiliated with Stremio.

Source: https://github.com/stremio-origin/webos
